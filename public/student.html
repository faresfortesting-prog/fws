🔒 What StudyStrike tracks

During a focus session we may use your webcam (presence detection only) and optional screen share (content compliance only). No video or screenshots are ever stored or transmitted to your instructor. Frames are analysed momentarily and discarded — only a privacy-safe summary (timestamp, event type, reason) is saved.

  • Instructors can see: your points, streaks, task progress, focus % and session summaries.
  • Instructors cannot see: raw webcam video, raw screenshots, your messages to others.
  • Verification events are summaries only (e.g. "face missing for 20s").
